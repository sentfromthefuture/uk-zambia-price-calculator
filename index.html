How the price is worked out

Landed cost = (UK price + UK postage) × exchange rate + Eastwing shipping + other expenses.
Recommended price = landed cost + target profit, rounded up to the next K50. Expected profit is that rounded price minus landed cost.

The auto-filled UK price is a benchmark (typical eBay UK sold price for a working, unlocked phone), not a guaranteed price — check the real listing, condition and battery, and type the actual price over it. The benchmark uplift only applies to the benchmark, never to a price you type.
