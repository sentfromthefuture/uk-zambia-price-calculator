How to use the eBay benchmark

The model selector is seeded with UK eBay completed-sale benchmarks. The default positioning adds 8% to the typical sold price to target the middle-to-upper end rather than the cheapest listings. You can change the percentage or overwrite the UK price with the exact listing you find. The estimator then adds shipping and your chosen profit.
